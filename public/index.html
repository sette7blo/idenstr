Public Relayskind:10002 · 10050

Your public relay policy: which relays Idenstr reads from, writes to, and receives DMs on. The draft policy is the source of truth. Publish signs the read/write list as kind:10002 and the DM relay list as kind:10050 (NIP-17), writes both to your private relay, then broadcasts them to your write relays. DM relays are where other people's clients drop your gift-wrapped DMs — keep them to a small, trusted set. Scan checks public relay state and mismatches. Your private relay is managed separately under Private relay.

Private Relayyour vault

Your private relay is the canonical vault for your signed events. It is local/LAN-only by default and is where Publish writes every event before it ever reaches public relays. Your other *str apps (Workstr, Feedstr) read from and sign into this same vault through Idenstr. This is a different store from your public relays: public relays distribute, the private relay owns.

Connection status and vault contents load automatically when you open this section. Save stores the private relay URL in .env; Refresh re-reads the vault on demand.

WalletNWC

Connect a lightning wallet over Nostr Wallet Connect (NIP-47). Idenstr can read your balance and pay invoices through the wallet — it never holds your funds. The connection string contains a spending secret: it is stored in .env alongside your nsec, never shown again, and never sent back to this page. Paste a fresh connection string to reconnect, or disconnect to remove it.

Backupsdb + vault

A backup is a single file bundling both your stores: the app database (profile, following, relay policy, tuning, API tokens, audit) and every signed event in your private relay. Restoring re-populates the vault. Your nsec is never included. Backups are saved to the server data volume; you can download, restore, or upload a backup from another instance.

  • App database — profile, following, relays, tuning, tokens
  • Private relay — all canonical signed events (the vault)
  • Never included — your nsec / private key

Tuningserver config

      
Parameters behind discovery, engagement scoring, and activity tiers. Defaults are sensible; adjust only if the tiers do not match how you read your follows.

      

        

          Discovery
          
How many candidates are fetched and ranked when discovering follows and relays, and how many make it onto the screen.

          
          
          
        

        

          Engagement scoring weights
          
How much each interaction type counts toward a follow's engagement score. Raise a weight to make that signal matter more.

          
          
          
          
        

        

          Engagement tier thresholds
          
Scores at or above these values place a follow in the High or Engaged tier; anything below is Low.

          
          
        

        

          Activity tiers (days since last activity)
          
Days since a follow last did anything. Each tier covers up to its value; past the Inactive limit a follow counts as dormant.
